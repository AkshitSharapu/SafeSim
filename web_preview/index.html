Standard Operating Procedure

SOP-FIRE-EXT-04

The P.A.S.S. Suppression Method

Memorize these four sequential actions before handling extinguishers.

P

PULL the Safety Pin

Snap the plastic tamper seal and remove the metal retaining pin on top of the handle assembly.

A

AIM Low at Base of Fire

Aim directly at the fuel source where combustion originates, not into high smoke flames.

S

SQUEEZE Operating Lever

Maintain a firm, steady squeeze on the lever to discharge pressurized extinguishing agent.

S

SWEEP Side to Side

Sweep smoothly across the burning zone until all embers are fully blanketed and suppressed.
