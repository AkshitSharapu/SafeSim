welcome: Welcome to Safe Sim. This virtual exercise demonstrates a simplified fire response. Select Start Training when you are ready.

alarm: First, raise the alarm. Follow the arrows to the red alarm station. Approach it, face it, and select Raise Alarm.

pickup: Collect the carbon dioxide extinguisher from its stand. Follow the arrows, face the cylinder, and select Pick Up.

pin: Pull the safety pin. Select Pull Pin, or press P on your keyboard.

approach: Follow the arrows to the turquoise operating mark. Keep the exit behind you.

suppress: Aim at the base of the fire. Hold Spray and sweep from side to side across all three fire zones. The aiming dot turns green when you are on target.

exit: The fire has been suppressed. Follow the arrows back to the exit to complete this exercise.

complete: Training complete. You raised the alarm, collected the extinguisher, removed the pin, and suppressed the fire. Review your results.

failed: The extinguisher is empty. Restart the exercise. Aim at the base of each fire zone and use controlled bursts.
